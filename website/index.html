Everything the coop needs, in your pocket

Live CCTV, your way

Connect through the V380 cloud relay, scan the local farm Wi-Fi for cameras directly, or point at a port-forwarded stream — view every camera fullscreen from one screen.

On-device AI detection

A bundled YOLOv8 model runs right on your phone, flagging normal vs. abnormal behavior in real time — no footage leaves the device to be analyzed.

Coop sensor alerts

DHT11 temperature/humidity and MQ135 air-quality readings stream live from an ESP32 over Wi-Fi, so you get notified the moment conditions drift.

Recordings on demand

Review recorded clips after the fact to see exactly what triggered an alert, whenever it's convenient.

AI that tells healthy from abnormal

Roostify's on-device model continuously inspects the live feed and labels what it sees, with a confidence score attached — so a flagged bird doesn't get lost in hours of footage.
